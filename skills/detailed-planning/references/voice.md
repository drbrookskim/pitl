# Voice & Tone Guide

The *structure* of the plan is crisp and scannable. The *narrative prose inside it* — the concept
story, the rationale, the "왜," the storytelling close — carries a distinct authorial voice. Match it.

## The voice in one line

A reflective practitioner-essayist who reasons from a concrete everyday observation up to a principle,
reaches across domains for analogies, stays humble about certainty, and ends on a question that hands
the thought back to the reader.

## Hallmarks (do)

- **Concrete → abstract.** Open a narrative beat with something tangible (an everyday scene, a product,
  a quote, a 사자성어) and climb to the principle. Not "이 제품은 가치가 있다" but a small observed
  thing that *earns* the claim.
- **Cross-domain analogy.** The author explains planning through farming (가지치기), bees (집단지성),
  watch movements vs AP chips, philosophy (칸트·비트겐슈타인·오컴), film, the brain, exercise. Reach for
  one apt analogy per plan to illuminate the core concept — sparingly, never forced.
- **Tentative, humble register.** "~이지 않을까", "~라고 생각한다", "~인 듯하다", "순전히 추측이지만".
  Plans are hypotheses, not decrees. State assumptions openly ("가정:…").
- **Value-centered closes.** Resolve back to the value question. End sections by asking whether the
  value truly serves the user.
- **A closing reflective question.** End the whole plan with a question that opens forward rather than
  a flat summary — "오늘 당신의 기획은 어떤 새로운 이름을 붙일 것인가?" style.
- **Reframing & dichotomies.** "장애가 아니라 무게다", "대체재인가 보완재인가", "보편적이되 차별적".
  Reframe the problem before solving it.
- **Crisp rhetorical beats.** Short lines for emphasis. "왜일까?" "맞다." A definition pulled in when a
  word matters.

## Anti-patterns (don't)

- No corporate buzzword soup, no breathless hype ("혁신적인 게임체인저!"). The author is wary of
  "무작정 제품의 막연한 우수성만 주장하는" copy.
- Don't over-claim certainty. Avoid "반드시 / 100% / 분명히 성공한다." Prefer "적절하게 / ~할 것이다."
- Don't design from the creator's convenience and dress it up as user value. Name the user's real
  situation honestly.
- Don't let analogies run the show — one well-placed analogy beats five decorative ones.
- Don't bury the value. Every narrative section should be traceable back to the core value.

## Calibration by language

- **Korean input → Korean prose** in this full voice (this is the native register of the corpus).
- **English input → English prose** that preserves the *spirit*: concrete-to-abstract, humble,
  analogy-aware, value-centered, closing question. Keep native planning terms (개념/시나리오/Must-have)
  where they are the natural word.

## Micro-examples

- Surface request reframed (Stage 0):
  > 표면적으로는 "알림 기능을 추가해 달라"는 요청이지만, 5번의 '왜'를 따라가 보면 사용자가 정말 원하는
  > 것은 '잊지 않을 것이라는 안심'이 아닐까. 그렇다면 만들 것은 알림이 아니라 '신뢰'일지도 모른다.

- Value close (Stage 3):
  > 기능을 더 얹고 싶은 유혹이 들 때마다 같은 질문으로 돌아온다. "사용자에게 주려는 가치가 정말 이게
  > 맞는가?" 이 질문을 통과하지 못하는 기능은, 아깝더라도 가지치기한다.

- Closing question (Stage 4):
  > 잘 만드는 것만큼 잘 이야기하는 것이 중요하다. 그렇다면 이 기획이 사용자의 하루에 남길 첫 문장은
  > 무엇이어야 할까?
